### How do M-Tech Solutions offline-first POS systems handle internet outages?
Our POS applications use local-first storage (IndexedDB/SQLite) and background sync workers. When internet connectivity drops, barcode scanning, billing, and thermal receipt printing continue seamlessly. When connectivity returns, queued transactions reconcile and sync automatically with the central cloud database.
